01

PLUMBING & HEATING / WEST MIDLANDS / 12 STAFF

Regional field operations, rebuilt around the job.

11 days → same dayinvoicing lag

Operational gapJobs came in by phone, went up on a whiteboard, then got re-keyed into a spreadsheet before billing. Invoices trailed finished work by around eleven days; roughly one job in twenty slipped through unbilled.

System implementedA single scheduling app with a missed-call auto-responder, day routing split across two depots, and one-tap invoicing the moment an engineer marks a job complete.

Reality checkSome engineers initially refused to close jobs in the app, so we added a voice-note fallback and a Monday reconciliation. Van-stock tracking now auto-deducts used parts and triggers reorders.

“The invoicing used to be the thing that kept me up. Now it goes out before the van’s back on the drive.”Co-owner

02

ACCOUNTANCY / ~600 SELF-ASSESSMENT CLIENTS

A cleaner route through peak-season records.

−80%peak-season admin

Operational gapChasing and checking client records over email swallowed most of January. Documents landed as photos, scans and forwarded threads, then staff re-filed mismatched formats by hand.

System implementedA secure portal with a per-client adaptive checklist, OCR validation on upload and a nudge sequence that chases missing items automatically.

CaveatOCR still trips on handwritten receipts and unusual P60 layouts, so about one document in ten routes to a person for a quick check. That manual check is intentional.

03

ECOMMERCE / OWN STORE + TWO MARKETPLACES

One inventory truth across three storefronts.

30/mo → 0oversells recorded

Operational gapOrders from three storefronts were merged into a warehouse pick-list by hand each morning. The lag caused around thirty cancellations a month and put one marketplace account at risk.

System implementedA stock layer that syncs inventory across all three channels in near real-time and raises purchase orders against dynamic reorder points.

Now in buildPhase two adds demand-based forecasting for seasonal ranges. It is live for the two biggest SKUs and being watched before widening.

“We haven’t oversold a single line since the spring.”Founder

04

DENTAL / THREE SITES / NHS + PRIVATE

Demand, reminders and standby capacity in sync.

15% → 7%no-show rate

Operational gapA stubborn no-show rate near fifteen percent, phone-only booking and recall reminders that went out late whenever the front desk was under pressure.

System implementedAn assistant that sends staged SMS reminders, lets patients book and reschedule online, and auto-fills late cancellations from a standby list.

CaveatStandby fill took two rounds to tune. Early on it double-booked a handful of slots before we tightened the confirmation window.

PRECISION ENGINEERING / CNC / 18 STAFF

Faster quotes for a busy local workshop.

3 days → <1hrquote turnaround

Operational gapBespoke quoting leaned on one estimator’s memory and a folder of old jobs. Slow turnaround was costing a small workshop time-sensitive work from local manufacturers and specialist vehicle restorers.

System implementedA quoting tool built on the workshop’s real material, machine and labour costs. It produces a consistent draft in under an hour and feeds accepted work into a live production board.

CaveatIt supports the estimator rather than replacing them. Every price is checked before it leaves the workshop.

“It took the guesswork out without taking the judgement away.”Works manager
